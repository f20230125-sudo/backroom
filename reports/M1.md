This gives 84 statements, 552 payout day-lines, and following-Wednesday bank receipts from 8 July through 7 October. No sales outside 1 July-30 September are invented.

Email share uses that classifier-document denominator. Invoice attachments carry a full InvoiceRecord on the containing email; duplicate attachment links identify the standalone original.

The read-only e-invoicing reference uses DXB/SHJ, so M3 needs an explicit mapping rather than treating DU as a new unplanted master-data error. Invoice VAT rates use percentage points (5); platform rates use fractions (0.25, 0.22, 0.28). Credit-note quantities and amounts are signed negative.
